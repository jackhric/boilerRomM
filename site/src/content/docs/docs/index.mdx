boilerRomM is a [Decky Loader](https://github.com/SteamDeckHomebrew/decky-loader) plugin that shows your
[RomM](https://romm.app) library as a store inside Steam's Game Mode. Pick a game and press **Download**: it appears
in your Steam library as a real entry with its artwork, and boilerRomM installs and configures the emulator that runs
it.

It has three parts, all inside Steam:

- A store: Discover, Browse, Top rated, New and Consoles, with search, filters, box art and trailers
- Steam library entries for the games you download, with Settings > Storage, Uninstall and Steam's Downloads page
- An emulator manager that installs nine emulators and the firmware they need from your RomM server
