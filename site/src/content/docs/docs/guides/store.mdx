## The store

Open it from Quick Access → **boilerRomM** → **Open store**. It has five tabs:

- **Discover**: a spotlight with trailers, a list of picks, a Featured shelf and your consoles
- **Browse**: your whole library as box art, with **All consoles** and **All genres** filters, a sort menu, and
  **Only on this device** to see what you have downloaded
- **Top rated** and **New** (new on your server)
- **Consoles**: one page per system in your library

**Search games** at the top searches the whole library. The refresh button reads the library from RomM again; the
store keeps no copy of its own, so what you see is what your server has.

## A game's page

Each game has a page with its artwork, a muted trailer when one is found, its description, reviews, genres and what
is on this device: its status, size and file. **More like this** suggests similar games from your library. The
**…** menu has **Remove from this device** and **Open in RomM**.

<Shot name="game" alt="A game page with box art, a trailer, the Download button, reviews and what is on this device" />

Descriptions, ratings and suggestions come from RomM. Artwork comes from SteamGridDB, and trailers are streamed with
[yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp).

## Downloads

Press **Download** on a game. Downloads run one at a time, in the order you queued them, and show:

- on the game's page and button
- on Steam's own **Downloads** page, next to your Steam games

<Shot name="downloads" alt="Steam's Downloads page with a boilerRomM game downloading" />

Each file downloads to a partial file and resumes where it stopped, so a download cut off by a restart carries on
by itself. **Stop download** stops it; **Resume** picks it up again.

Games are saved in the ROM folder set in **Settings > Storage**, one subfolder per system.

## In your Steam library

A downloaded game is a non-Steam shortcut in your library, with box art, a banner, a logo and an icon from
SteamGridDB. Pressing **Play** runs it in its emulator.

<Shot name="library" alt="Steam's library, Non-Steam tab, with boilerRomM games and their box art" />

It also:

- appears in Steam's **Settings > Storage** with its size
- has **Uninstall** in its menu, which removes its files from the device
- shows its files under **Properties → Installed Files**

:::note
boilerRomM is in development: most emulators have not launched a real game through it yet. See
[Emulators and BIOS](../emulators/#status).
:::

<Shot name="storage" alt="Steam's Storage settings listing boilerRomM games by size" />

Everything boilerRomM changes in Steam applies only to its own games, and unloading the plugin undoes it.
